How It Works

Audio Ingestion
Loads the audio file, forces mono channel, and resamples to 22kHz for consistent processing.
Spectrogram Analysis
Converts the loudest 2-second audio window into a high-resolution 128x128 mel spectrogram image.
CNN Inference
Our proprietary neural network classifies the spectral patterns to distinguish real from AI-generated voices.
